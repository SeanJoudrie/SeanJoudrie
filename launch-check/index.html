How it works

  2. 2

    Answer two or three questions

    What you built, what it's about, and one detail if your category has extra rules.

  3. 3

    Get your gaps and checklist

    The biggest problems first, then every step in order, each with the reason it matters.

What it looks for

  • The legal pages app stores ask for, like a privacy policy and terms of service
  • How your users' data is stored, and who can read it
  • Store requirements that often catch first-timers, like privacy details, account deletion and a login for reviewers
  • Extra rules for sensitive categories such as finance, health and apps for kids

What it doesn't do

Launch Check tells you what to do and why. It doesn't write code, teach you to build or judge your design. It isn't a lawyer either: it tells you which legal pages you need, not what they should say.

How do you want to describe it?

  1. Copy this prompt

    For your AI chat
    Write a short summary of the app we built together, for a launch readiness check. Use plain language. Don't include any code, API keys or passwords. Cover: - What the app does and who it's for - Where it will be released (App Store, Google Play, web) - How people sign up or log in, if they do - What data it collects or stores (for example names, emails, locations, payments, health details) - Where that data is stored, and whether it's encrypted - Outside services it uses (for example payments, analytics, login, AI) - Whether it charges money or has subscriptions If you're not sure about something, say so. Keep it under 200 words.
